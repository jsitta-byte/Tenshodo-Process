## Claim types

Examples:

- capability;
- customer outcome;
- comparative;
- quantitative;
- security/privacy;
- compliance;
- experience/tenure;
- market/category;
- testimonial;
- case-study.

## Approval states

- draft;
- evidence_ready;
- proposed;
- approved_for_use;
- restricted;
- retired.

## Rules

- No material claim should be marked approved without supporting evidence and valid adoption authority.
- Quantitative claims should identify population/time period and calculation source.
- Comparative claims should identify the comparison basis.
- Testimonials/case claims should preserve customer permission requirements.
- Security, compliance, legal, privacy, and guarantee-like statements should route to appropriate specialist review.
- Revalidate claims when the underlying evidence, offer, market, or policy changes materially.
