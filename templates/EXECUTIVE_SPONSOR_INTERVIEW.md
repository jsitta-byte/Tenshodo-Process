# Executive Sponsor Interview Guide

## Purpose

Establish the mandate, boundaries, and transformation priorities before broad discovery.

This is not an org-design interview.

## 1. Why now?

- What changed or became painful enough to justify this engagement?
- What would make this engagement materially successful?
- What would make it fail even if we produced good documentation?
- Which outcomes matter in the next 90 / 180 / 365 days?

## 2. Current operating pain

- Where does work most often stall?
- Where is context lost when people change roles or conversations?
- Which recurring work depends too heavily on individual memory?
- Where do teams repeatedly re-enter or reconcile the same information?
- What changes tend to create unexpected downstream problems?
- Which decisions are slower or less reliable than they should be?

## 3. Authority

- What decisions can the consulting team make independently?
- What may we build or propose but not adopt?
- Who can adopt company-wide process changes?
- Who can authorize changes to systems, access, spend, contracts, employment, or risk posture?
- Where is authority currently unclear?

## 4. Evidence and systems

- Which systems should we trust for workforce, finance, sales, product, customer, system access, policies, and process truth?
- Which important records are known to be stale or incomplete?
- What shadow systems or spreadsheets should we expect to find?
- What repositories/workspaces should be connected first?

## 5. Organization

- Where do titles poorly describe the work people actually do?
- Are there functions the company needs but does not formally staff?
- Which people are overloaded as informal hubs?
- Which responsibilities are especially unclear or contested?

## 6. AI and automation

- Where is AI already being used?
- Which AI use is sanctioned versus informal?
- Where would AI assistance be valuable but risky?
- What should an AI role conversation never be allowed to decide?
- What level of human review is expected for consequential outputs?

## 7. Transformation tolerance

- How much operating change can the company absorb at once?
- Which areas are too sensitive for an early pilot?
- Where could we prove value quickly with relatively low risk?
- Which leaders/employees are likely to become strong stewards of the new operating model?

## 8. Consultancy boundary

- What information must never leave client systems?
- Are there repositories/systems the consulting team should not access?
- What retention requirements apply to consultant-created artifacts?
- What intellectual property may be generalized into sanitized methodology, if any?

## Output

The interview should produce:

- engagement mandate;
- success criteria;
- authority boundary;
- access/confidentiality boundary;
- initial priority hypotheses;
- candidate high-value pilots;
- unresolved sponsor decisions.
