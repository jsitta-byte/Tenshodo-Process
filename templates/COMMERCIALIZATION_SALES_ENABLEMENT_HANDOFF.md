## 8. Authority boundaries

Sales may not independently change:

- offer scope;
- pricing outside approved rules;
- contractual commitments;
- security/compliance representations;
- validated claims;
- delivery guarantees.

## 9. Field feedback loop

Capture:

- objections heard;
- confusing messages;
- winning messages;
- lost-opportunity reasons;
- buyer-language changes;
- requested capabilities;
- proof gaps.

Route durable findings back to marketing/product/revenue owners rather than editing source truth ad hoc.
