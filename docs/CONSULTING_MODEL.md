# Consulting Model

## Mission

Tenshodo Process exists so a transformation team can repeatedly help organizations become durable, inspectable, dependency-aware, and increasingly AI-enabled without making the client dependent on the consultants' private memory.

The consultancy should leave behind a better operating system, not merely a collection of recommendations.

## What we sell

The core service is **organizational operating-system transformation**.

Depending on the client, engagements may include:

- durable work-state architecture;
- GitHub-backed management/control-plane design;
- SharePoint, Google Workspace, or other working-plane integration;
- process inventory and dependency mapping;
- change-governance design;
- AI role/function incubation;
- agent/prompt governance;
- executive and functional operating models;
- cross-functional change orchestration;
- human handoff and capability transfer.

## Delivery philosophy

### Build with the client, not around the client

The consultants may initially create structure faster than the client can absorb it.

That is acceptable if:

- authority boundaries are respected;
- work is transparent;
- durable state is client-accessible;
- every consultant-built function is designed for human takeover;
- no essential operating knowledge remains in consultant-only conversation history.

### Functions can precede owners

A client may need a capability before it has a person with the right title or skill.

The method permits a governed AI role conversation or consulting team to incubate that function while preserving the distinction between:

- target accountability;
- current execution;
- adoption authority.

### The engagement should survive consultant departure

A successful engagement passes the "bus/exit test":

If the Tenshodo team left tomorrow, could the client determine:

- what is complete;
- what is in progress;
- what evidence exists;
- what decisions are pending;
- who can act;
- what the next exact action is?

If not, the operating system is not durable enough.

## Consultancy engagement artifacts

Each client should normally receive its own authorized control-plane repository or equivalent durable system.

The client repository should contain:

- current state;
- transformation plan;
- runbook;
- zero-context handoff;
- authority model;
- decision records;
- process/capability registries;
- worklogs;
- role-conversation charters where used;
- dependency/impact structures;
- exact next actions.

The public Tenshodo Process repository supplies patterns and templates, never private client state.

## Commercial evolution

The consultancy can mature from:

1. founder-led transformation;
2. small expert transformation team;
3. repeatable multi-client delivery;
4. domain-specialist pods using the same durable methodology;
5. licensed/internal client operating model supported by continuing advisory work.

The method should be designed so quality does not depend on one individual consultant being present forever.
