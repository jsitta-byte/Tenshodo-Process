# Playbook 03 — AI Role Incubation

## Use when

A client needs a function before it has:

- a clear human owner;
- a mature job description;
- sufficient internal understanding of the function;
- capacity to staff it.

## Concept

Create a chartered role conversation.

The conversation adopts the function's:

- mission;
- perspective;
- source rules;
- work queue;
- output contract;
- checkpoint discipline;
- escalation rules.

It does not become the executive.

## Allowed work

Typical build/propose work:

- research;
- analysis;
- inventory;
- process architecture;
- documentation;
- dependency mapping;
- draft controls;
- recommendations;
- work planning;
- testing;
- evaluation;
- durable checkpointing.

## Binding authority

Keep human or otherwise explicitly delegated.

A role conversation does not automatically:

- hire/fire;
- set pay;
- spend;
- sign contracts;
- grant access;
- accept material risk;
- adopt its own policy.

## Lifecycle

1. AI-incubated
2. human-supervised
3. human-led with AI copilot
4. formally staffed/delegated
5. retained as support or retired

## Handoff objective

The future human should inherit a working function, not a blank page.
