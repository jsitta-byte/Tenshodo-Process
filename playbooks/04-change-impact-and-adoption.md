# Playbook 04 — Change Impact and Adoption

## Use when

A local idea may have cross-functional consequences.

Example:

> We are selling X successfully. There may be an opportunity to launch Y.

## Lifecycle

1. **Suggest** — capture problem/opportunity and proposed change.
2. **Triage** — classify change and identify current owner.
3. **Map impact** — identify candidate downstream objects.
4. **Decide** — accountable owners determine actual required scope.
5. **Plan** — create linked implementation work.
6. **Implement** — execute in bounded checkpoints.
7. **Validate** — confirm affected systems/processes/controls are ready.
8. **Adopt** — activate at a defined cutover.
9. **Observe** — measure and revisit.

## Critical distinction

A dependency graph identifies **candidate blast radius**.

It does not grant permission to change every dependent object.

## Candidate impact classes

Depending on the client:

- product/SKU;
- pricing/economics;
- accounting;
- CRM/sales;
- marketing;
- brand;
- contracts;
- operations;
- onboarding/support;
- data/reporting;
- training;
- systems/integrations;
- controls;
- security;
- legal/risk.

## Definition of success

A local improvement can move quickly without creating hidden downstream breakage or uncontrolled enterprise-wide change.
